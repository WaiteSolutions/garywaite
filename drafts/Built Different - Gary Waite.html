04 — The library

A collector's brain, shelved.

The collection is the tell. Every book is a pattern I wanted to own — biotech adjacent, marketing canon, wrestling history, and the weird spines nobody else keeps. Copywriting is just librarianship with a deadline: know where everything is, pull the right volume, quote it precisely.

If we work together, expect references. Expect the footnote. Expect someone who actually read the thing.

05 — The mat

USA Wrestling coach.

Friday nights and weekend tournaments, I'm on the mat coaching kids through the sport that teaches everything my career did: position before panic, pressure with purpose, and getting up one more time than you were put down.

Coaching is also the purest form of what I do for clients and talent — read the person, diagnose the situation they can't see, and put them in position to win. The uniform changes. The job doesn't.
