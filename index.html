03 / beyond scope

Notes from the corner.

Coaching youth wrestling is one way I give back in North Texas. I want young wrestlers to earn the confidence that comes from sticking with a hard move until it clicks.

On the mat, discipline looks like showing up, putting in the reps, and helping your teammate get better. Coaching taught me to watch where someone gets stuck and give them one next move they can use.

  • Position before panic

    Control the center before your opponent forces a reaction. In a campaign, choose the audience and the problem you’ll own before the launch clock starts.

  • Coach the one in front of you

    One cue can click for one wrestler and confuse the next. Segment by what readers already know and what they still need to hear.

  • Review the loss

    Rewatch the exchange that cost you the match. In a funnel, find where people stopped and test one change at that step.

  • Pressure with purpose

    Pressure earns its keep when it creates an opening. Give a reader a concrete reason to act and make the next step easy to take.

  • Give them a reason to return

    On the mat, I care about who comes back the next season. In ecommerce, track the second purchase and give customers a useful reason to make it.
